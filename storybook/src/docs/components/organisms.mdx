# Organisms

Complex UI sections composed of molecules and/or atoms. An organism forms a recognizable, standalone section of the interface — a piece of the page that could be lifted out and still make sense on its own. Organisms often have their own internal logic and layout.

**Use for:** header, footer, main navigation, mega menu, breadcrumb, hero, card grid, article teaser list, search results block, language selector, sidebar, cookie banner, modal, form, table.
