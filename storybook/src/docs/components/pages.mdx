# Pages

A specific instance of a template filled with real, representative content. Pages are used for review, testing, and stakeholder sign-off. They are not reusable components — they are examples that prove the system works end-to-end.

**Use for:** homepage with actual copy and images, a specific article detail page, a filled-in contact form page, a search results page with real query results.
